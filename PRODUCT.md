# Product

## Register

product

## Platform

web

## Users

The primary audience is developers and product stakeholders evaluating how agentic engineering workflows behave in practice. They need a concrete product surface and enough visible evidence to understand where agents participate, what they produce, and where human control remains.

## Product Purpose

This repository demonstrates multiple agentic development workflows around a playable Sudoku application. Success means a reviewer can use the product, inspect issue-driven planning, observe pull-request browser validation, and understand agent-assisted failure diagnosis without mistaking advisory automation for autonomous authority.

## Positioning

The Sudoku app is the shared test surface; transparent workflow evidence is the showcase.

## Brand Personality

Technical, trustworthy, and transparent. Language is direct and specific about what agents can and cannot do.

## Anti-references

Avoid flashy AI-demo styling: gradients, glow effects, theatrical automation, exaggerated claims, and interfaces that imply the agent has authority it does not possess.

## Design Principles

1. Show the boundary: agent actions, permissions, and human review points must be explicit.
2. Practice credibility: the product itself must be usable and thoroughly tested.
3. Prefer evidence over claims: surface checks, artifacts, and diagnoses rather than promotional copy.
4. Keep the task primary: game controls remain familiar and the workflow story never obstructs play.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Provide deliberate baseline accessibility: keyboard-operable Sudoku, visible focus, semantic labels, readable contrast, comfortable touch targets, and screen-reader status feedback. No formal conformance certification is claimed.
